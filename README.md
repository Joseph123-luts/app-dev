# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: No Way Home

## Introduction

One of my favorite movies is **Spider-Man: No Way Home**. It is an exciting superhero movie filled with action, adventure, and emotional moments.

## About the Movie

The story follows *Peter Parker*, whose identity as Spider-Man becomes public. He seeks help from Doctor Strange, but things go wrong and lead to unexpected challenges.

### Why I Like This Movie

- It has exciting action scenes.
- It teaches the importance of responsibility.
- It shows friendship, sacrifice, and courage.

### My Favorite Characters

1. Spider-Man (Peter Parker)
2. Doctor Strange
3. MJ

### My Favorite Quote

> With great power comes great responsibility.
